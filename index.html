Proposal for the Survival Strategy Competition Program 'The Professional'

Team Members: 박주상, 김두현, 강유준, 이윤승
GitHub: joooosang.github.io

1. Introduction

This project was conducted to design the first original content for 'StreamKUS'. Rather than relying on the production team's intuition, we analyzed actual viewer text data to identify 'professionalism' and 'psychological strategy' as the core success factors that drive audience enthusiasm. Based on these insights, we propose a new program format titled 'The Professional'.

2. Data Collection

  • Data Source: User reviews and ratings from the global review platform IMDb.
  • Selected Programs: Three representative programs across different genres: cooking (Hell's Kitchen), performing arts (America's Got Talent), and action/survival (Survivor).
  • Selection Criteria: To ensure data reliability, we targeted long-running programs with a minimum of 100 reviews and at least 50 total episodes (spanning at least 5 seasons).
  • Justification: The selected programs—Survivor, America's Got Talent, and Hell's Kitchen—are highly popular, long-running shows that have accumulated extensive datasets over many years. Analyzing these large-scale datasets allows us to statistically substantiate the elements that viewers consistently favor across genres, such as professionalism and strategy, as well as the elements they reject, such as artificial production.
  • Data Scale: Hundreds of unstructured review texts were collected and utilized for the analysis.

3. Text Mining Methods

Data Preprocessing

We standardized the sentiment labels of each dataset into a uniform format and performed data cleaning to remove unnecessary variables, such as seasons and ratings, that did not align with our analytical goals. Additionally, independently collected files in various formats were integrated into a unified analysis environment, and missing values and noise were filtered out to ensure data consistency.

Sentiment Analysis

We applied a deep learning-based model to quantify the sentiment within the review texts. Moving beyond simple classification, we implemented a weighted sum score method to transform unstructured text into precise numerical data.

Topic Modeling and Keyword Extraction

Using Codex and BERTopic technologies, we extracted core themes from the extensive reviews. We mapped the positive and negative factors repeatedly mentioned by viewers in each genre to derive Top 10 charts.

4. Findings

Positive Factors:

  • Skill and Realism: Viewers showed a highly positive response to Hell's Kitchen's 'professional chef competitions (48.6%)' and the 'liveliness of the kitchen (48.6%)'.
  • Psychological Tension: Audiences were enthusiastic about Survivor's 'gameplay completion (64.3%)' and 'tribal council psychology (57.4%)'.

Negative Factors:

  • Incompetence: Viewers expressed the strongest aversion to Hell's Kitchen's 'unskilled and incompetent participants (41.2%)'.
  • Artificial Production: Audiences identified Survivor's 'excessive production intervention (33.6%)' and AGT's 'forced tears and emotional manipulation (20.7%)' as major points of dissatisfaction.

5. Content Proposals

Participant Composition (Elimination of Incompetence)

Based on data evidence (41.2% negative for Hell's Kitchen), the cast will consist entirely of seasoned professionals with over 10 years of experience in their respective fields. We aim for high satisfaction (95.1%), similar to AGT's top stages, through "flawless performances by the best."

Autonomous System (Blocking Production Intervention)

Reflecting the Survivor data (33.6% negative), the authority to eliminate participants will be delegated entirely to the contestants themselves. This induces a pure strategic battle free from artificial intervention.

Subdued Production (Focusing on the Essence)

Incorporating feedback on AGT's 'excessive reactions (41.4%)', we will eliminate melodramatic editing and maximize a documentary-style realism that focuses on the craftsmen's technical skills.

Symbolism (Providing Catharsis)

Mirroring Hell's Kitchen's success factor, the 'black jacket (31.4% positive)', we will award specially crafted uniforms and tools to survivors to visualize professional authority.

6. Conclusion

Summary of Core Findings and Proposals

This project numerically demonstrated the success formulas and failure factors of survival programs by analyzing IMDb text review data. The analysis revealed that viewers responded highly positively to the overwhelming professionalism and sophisticated strategies of the participants, while showing strong aversion to participant incompetence and artificial production interventions. Based on these findings, we propose a new content model, 'The Professional', which combines the recruitment of seasoned experts with an autonomous council system to reflect the core needs of the audience.

Limitations of the Analysis

There were practical limitations during the research process. First, due to a lack of reviews for specific episodes, we had to adopt an integrated program-level analysis rather than the initially planned episode-specific detailed analysis. Second, utilizing data solely from the single platform IMDb introduces the possibility that the results are skewed toward the tendencies of a specific user base.

Future Directions

It is necessary to collect additional data from YouTube trending features and social media platforms to dynamically reflect real-time public reactions. Furthermore, a comparative analysis with popular domestic programs like 'Culinary Class Wars' could help derive localized strategies tailored to different cultural contexts. Ultimately, introducing episode-level time-series sentiment analysis to track text characteristics at specific viewership fluctuation points will provide more concrete, data-driven production guidelines.
